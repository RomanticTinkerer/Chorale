What does your data contain?

The data in question is the one you provide when registering on different sites. This includes but is not limited to:

  1. 01 Profile Picture
  2. 02 Full Name
  3. 03 Date of Birth
  4. 04 Residence
  5. 05 Current (or past) workplaces
  6. 06 Phone Numbers
  7. 07 Affiliations

Why does it concern you?

Your data is commonly

sold

to advertisers for continued marketing, to show relevant ads. It is tracked, butchered and has a

95% chance it ends up in the wrong hands.

It is also used to train language models (LLMs) and image-recognition systems (computer vision).

Okay, so how can I stop it?

You can request data removal from certain applications but there are some third-party tools that guide in data removal from the wide Internet. However, not all the approaches are plausible and do not work with 100% certainty.

What is the Chorale Foundation?

It is a non-profit based in India to educate people and to take a stand against mindless data collection and corporate greed. We aim to propagate the message of more privacy-focused approaches to take to navigate this world.
